# Hustle Through — Full Tier & Level Design Doc

**Setting:** Glasgow (home base) + Ayrshire coast/Ayr (personal/finale missions) + regional towns via train
**Structure:** 70 levels across 13 ranks. One job at a time. Jobs pulled via business card rack spin.

---

*Tone: petty, local, low stakes. Establishing home turf and family ties.*

## Rank 14: HEAD HUSTLER (Level 70) — Finale

**Location: Ayr, coastal — the same beach/town established as the player's personal/family location throughout the game.**

**"The Rack Has No More Cards"**

The player is summoned to Ayr — the place they've only ever known as home for family errands, babysitting runs, birthdays. DeadmanXXXII and V00D00 are waiting, anonymous masks on, no crew, no crowd. They reveal they've been watching and testing the player since the first unnamed card at Level 24.

Final task: a last job with no card, no phone call — DeadmanXXXII and V00D00 hand the briefing directly. On completion, both men remove their masks (or choose not to — player-facing narrative choice on how much mystery to preserve) and formally confer the title. The player receives their own mask, engraved or styled with "Head Hustler," and the rack — the physical object that started every job in the game — is retired on screen.

**Reward:** Full narrative epilogue, Head Hustler cosmetic set, no further cash payout — rank and story closure are the reward, preserving the "you can't buy the ending" rule established early on.
